## Who maintains the app
Salty Panda LLC maintains PocketWork, an independent iOS companion for OpenWork. Support is available at support@saltypanda.com and through the public GitHub Issues page for the PocketWork iOS repository. Security concerns can be reported through that repository's private security-reporting option.

## Your computer and model providers
PocketWork connects to the computer you pair and the workspaces you approve there. Chat messages, question answers, selected attachments and requested changes are sent to that computer over authenticated HTTPS. OpenWork processes them using the model provider and services configured on your computer. A provider can receive the prompt, relevant conversation context, selected files and other context used by OpenWork. Local models and cloud providers handle data differently. Their privacy and retention terms apply to the data they receive. Tools running on the computer can contact other configured services.

## What stays on the phone
The phone stores its pairing credential in the device Keychain, available while the device is unlocked and restricted to that device. It keeps unsent text, question and skill drafts, attachment copies and information needed to recover unconfirmed actions in app storage. Draft and transfer storage uses iOS file protection and is excluded from backups. Appearance and compact Activity preferences are stored locally using system preferences. Chat messages, search results, file lists and diffs are displayed in memory rather than saved as a transcript by this app. Downloaded results and preview copies are temporary local files. Selected photos are normalized before upload to remove original metadata. Filenames and transfer metadata are sent with selected files.

## Retention and removal
Drafts and unfinished actions can remain until resolved or removed. Download and preview copies expire through the app's cleanup process. Data on this phone provides three separate controls. Clear unsent drafts removes unsent chat text, question answers and skill drafts. It keeps in-progress and unconfirmed action details and selected attachments so an uncertain change cannot quietly be repeated. Remove downloaded copies removes this app's result and preview files; it keeps selected attachments and copies shared to other apps. Reset all local data removes pairing, drafts, unfinished action records, selected attachment copies, downloaded copies and the app's appearance and Activity preferences. If storage or Keychain removal fails, the app reports the problem instead of claiming everything was removed.

Reset and Forget this computer attempt to revoke this phone's access on the paired computer. If the computer is offline or revocation cannot be confirmed, its device grant may remain. Revoke the phone in OpenWork when the computer is online. Forgetting a computer alone does not erase all local drafts. These controls do not delete chats or original files on your computer. Data already sent to OpenWork, a provider or a sharing destination must be managed there.

## Permissions
Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a pairing code. You can paste a pairing code without granting camera access. Native Photos and Files pickers let you select content; the app does not request broad photo-library access. The current beta also requires Tailscale on both devices. Tailscale is a separate product with its own account and privacy practices.

## Diagnostics and websites
PocketWork does not include an advertising, analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDK. Development builds can record timing measurements in the local system log; they do not intentionally record chat text or tokens. Apple may collect TestFlight diagnostics under its own settings and terms. Privacy and support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. Opening those pages, GitHub Issues, Apple's terms or a sharing destination sends the usual browser or app request to that service. GitHub and those services apply their own policies.

## Support requests
GitHub Issues are public. Do not post pairing codes, tokens, private conversations, personal files or model credentials. Information you choose to put in a support request is handled through GitHub and may remain visible there. Use private security reporting for a security concern.
