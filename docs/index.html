PocketWork

Your computer. Your chats.

PocketWork is an independently maintained iOS companion for OpenWork, maintained by Salty Panda LLC. The current private beta requires the tested OpenWork preview and Tailscale on your phone and computer. Public App Store availability is not announced.

Help and privacy

Read the support page for connection steps and safe ways to report a problem. The privacy page explains where your data goes and how to remove local copies.
