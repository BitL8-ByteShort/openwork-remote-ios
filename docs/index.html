PocketWork

Your computer. Your chats.

PocketWork is an independent iPhone and iPad companion for OpenCode v2, maintained by Salty Panda LLC. The native client uses OpenCode's built-in device pairing and Remote access. It requires a compatible, running OpenCode server. Public App Store availability has not been announced.
