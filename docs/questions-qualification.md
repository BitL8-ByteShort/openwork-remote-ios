# Questions and forms

Pending OpenCode questions appear above the conversation, outside compact Activity. Open the question, choose supported options or enter an answer, then explicitly submit. Closing the sheet keeps its local answer draft. **Dismiss question** cancels the pending request on the computer.

The candidate supports the native form types its typed adapter can validate. Unsupported, changed or external-browser steps hand off to the computer. A question that changes while you answer needs fresh review. Answering is separate from granting a tool approval.

Before an answer that shares data, review the reported AI-sharing context. Review approval alone does not submit. Drafts and uncertain answers stay scoped to their original connection/project/chat/folder. Unconfirmed submission is not automatically repeated after reconnect or relaunch. Check the actual computer state before retrying.

Synthetic native state/UI cases and owned Mac/NUC form settlement checks exist. They do not establish the entire real agent-question lifecycle or physical VoiceOver acceptance. Earlier OpenWork question screenshots are historical and do not qualify the native release. See [compatibility](opencode-compatibility.md) and [accessibility](accessibility-qualification.md).
